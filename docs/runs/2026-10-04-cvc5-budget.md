# P1-033 cvc5 budget: gitextensions-8522 (2026-10-04)

Question: which `--rlimit` should cvc5 get, and how many of the pair's `timeout` Unknowns does asking
it leave?

**Answer: `--rlimit` 2,000,000. With it, 103 of the 164 results are still Unknown(timeout), against
140 without cvc5.** The 37 fewer are 8 more Divergent, 27 more Unknown(abstraction) and 2 more
Unknown(opaque). No pair becomes Equivalent, as P1-025 found.

At 2,000,000 cvc5 answers 58 of the 95 scripts it is sent. A quarter of that limit answers 34. Four
times it answers 57, one fewer, and takes longer. So 2,000,000 is the smallest of the three limits
that gets the answers there are to get.

It does not meet ADR 0050 decision 5 in full. Of the 37 scripts cvc5 gives up on at 2,000,000, its
resource limit ends 14 and the wall-clock limit ends 23. The clock ends 24% of all scripts sent, and
those results can differ between runs and machines. At 500,000 the limit ends 51 of 60, at the cost
of 24 answers.

49 of the `timeout` Unknowns send cvc5 nothing at any limit: the query holds a constant array with a
symbolic default that is used as a whole, so it is not sent, or the timeout is on a later rung.

## Setup
- Pair: human, gitextensions/gitextensions PR #8522, legacy 3f4ed21998af, modern 5190ba5c1a5f
  (`tools/corpus/pairs.csv`).
- Input: the 164 results with `unknownReason: timeout` in P2-076's `full` run of that pair (equiv
  `8e29816`, `runs/20261002-1839-full-after-again`), the input P1-025 used. All 164 identities still
  match a pair.
- equiv: branch `P1-033-cvc5-rung-1-queries` at `01cf81e`, win-x64, Release. Default config: bound
  3, `resourceLimit` 5,000,000, `timeoutMs` 60,000, which is also cvc5's wall-clock limit.
- cvc5 1.4.1, release `cvc5-1.4.1`, `cvc5-Win64-x86_64-static.zip`, SHA-256
  `1aaafe93484b5ed2cc8a74c2079242e85f160044afea647fadd3e794f7f7e50d`, run with `--arrays-exp`,
  `--rlimit` and `--tlimit`.
- Tool: `tools/spikes/cvc5-budget/`. It loads both solutions through the production frontend and hands
  each pair to `Z3Backend.Verify`, the call `compare` makes, once with no second solver and once per
  limit with `Cvc5Solver` as `VerificationOptions.Solver`. Nothing after the backend ran (no
  contracts pass, no IL fallback, no `--execute`), so this is not a `full` run of the pair.
- Machine: one Windows box, 24 logical cores, six pairs at a time. Loading and lowering took 284 s;
  the whole measurement took 4,382 s.

## What does not repeat
- `GitCommandsTests.Git.Tag.GitTagControllerTest::Setup()` is Divergent without cvc5 and at
  8,000,000, and Unknown(timeout) at the other two limits. cvc5 is asked only after Z3 gives up, so
  this is Z3 answering a query in one run and not in another, which P2-050 saw and P2-100 owns.
- `GitExtensions.Program::HandleConfigurationException(System.Configuration.ConfigurationException)`
  and `GitUI.CommandsDialogs.FormBrowse::FillUserShells(string)` are Unknown(abstraction) at
  2,000,000 and Unknown(timeout) at 8,000,000. A larger limit cannot lose an answer by itself; the
  wall-clock limit ended those scripts in the later pass, with six pairs running at once.
- One pass per configuration was run. The counts above are one sample each.

## Against P1-025
P1-025 measured 9 Divergent, 27 Unknown(abstraction) and 4 unsatisfiable `divergence` queries from
cvc5 at 60 s with no resource limit, and lost 19 satisfiable answers because Z3 could not complete
the model. Here, at 2,000,000, cvc5 gave 54 satisfiable and 4 unsatisfiable answers, and 40 results
have a query whose answer was kept. The tool did not record which satisfiable answers the read-back
dropped, so the size of that loss is not measured here; that it is still there is what the gap
between 58 and 40 suggests. The ticket's note on decoding cvc5's functions stands as a follow-up.
