# P1-037 Lean proofs of hard queries spike: gitextensions-8522 (2026-10-04)

Question: can a rung 1 query the solvers give up on be closed as a Lean theorem, by tactics that
need no insight or by a model whose proof Lean checks?

**Answer: a model can write a proof Lean's kernel accepts for a real query, and it decides nothing
a solver does not.** Of the 142 queries, 139 translate and 121 elaborate within ten minutes. The
scaffold alone closes none. A model closes 2 of the 5 it was asked: both are among the four
`divergence` queries cvc5 proved unsatisfiable, both are proved by Z3 itself in the positional
encoding (P1-034), and neither pair becomes Equivalent. On the three queries no solver decides that
the model could read, it wrote no proof and argued each time that the query is satisfiable.

**The number of `timeout` Unknowns a kernel-checked proof decides: 1 of 142, 0 by the scaffold
alone and 1 with the model; a second is closed with the model through `bv_decide`, which Lean's
kernel does not check alone. Mean cost of a closed proof: 781 seconds and 541,777 tokens.**

## Setup

- Queries: the 142 rung 1 queries of P1-025, in the positional form P1-034 wrote

  "solvers" below is that run's best answer for the positional query from Z3, cvc5 or Bitwuzla.
- Tool: `tools/spikes/lean-vc/` (about 850 lines of Python). Its README has the operator table.
- Lean 4.34.1 (commit 5045d0056413, Apache-2.0), installed by elan 4.2.4 under `.corpus/elan`; the

- Machine: one Windows box, 24 logical cores, 8 to 12 queries at a time, other work beside it.

## Method
**Translation.** One theorem a file: each declared sort a type variable with decidable equality,
each constant a binder, each assertion `A` a hypothesis `A = true`, the conclusion `False`. That is
`not (assertions and query)`, curried. Arrays are functions and `store` is a function update. A
`Bool` term stays a Lean `Bool`.

**Scaffold.** Each of `bv_decide`, `grind`, `simp_all`, `simp_all <;> grind`, `bv_omega` and
`omega` as the whole proof, for 120 seconds beyond what the statement takes to elaborate. `grind`
and `bv_decide` split the per-path boolean structure themselves; there is no separate splitting
step and one hole, the whole goal.

**Model.** The theorem and the last error, at most five rounds, each answered with Lean's error. A
query some solver answers satisfiable is not sent (the ticket: it is not proved by anyone), and
neither is a statement over 1,500,000 characters.

**Admission.** Lean compiles the generated statement followed by the proof, with no error and no
`sorry`; the proof text holds no command; `#print axioms` lists at most `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. A `bv_decide` proof also depends on an axiom of its own
(`q._native.bv_decide.ax_…`): its SAT certificate is checked by compiled code, not by the kernel.
Such a proof is reported apart and is not called kernel-checked.

## Self-test (criterion 2)
`--self-test` passes, 10 of 10: five unsatisfiable queries proved through the scaffold and five
satisfiable ones whose Z3 model, written as Lean definitions, makes Lean evaluate the assertions to
`true`. Division and remainder by zero, signed and unsigned comparison, shifts past the width, a
store and a select, and an uninterpreted function are each in one of each kind. The first query
equates every bit-vector operator on edge operands with Z3's own value for it. With two mappings
swapped on purpose (`bvsrem` as `smod`, `bvudiv` as Lean's `/`) two of the ten fail.

## Positive control (criterion 3)
Trivet's `(x /u y) * y <=u x` at 32 bits, written as the query that says it fails and put through
the same translator, scaffold and model loop. Every scaffold tactic fails; `bv_decide` alone, given
a 120-second SAT budget, times out, as Alive2 does in the paper. The model's second proof is
admitted with the three standard axioms: 2 rounds, 28 seconds, 15,934 tokens. The paper's mean is
684 seconds. The pipeline reproduces the paper's result on its example.

## What the answers are worth
- ADR 0005's objection, that a model writes proofs slowly and unreliably, holds for the slowness
  and not for the rest: both proofs it attempted on true theorems were admitted, in one and two
  rounds.
- It adds nothing to what P1-038 gives. Z3 proves the first query in 36 ms and the second in 88 ms
  in the positional form; Lean and the model take 554 and 1,009 seconds.
- Most of these timeouts are not theorems. 110 of the 121 that elaborate have a satisfiable answer
  from some solver, and the model says the same of the three undecided ones it read.
- Lean's automation does not reach this size. Nothing that needs no insight closes a query with
  hundreds of hypotheses over uninterpreted functions, so every proof costs a model call of several
  hundred thousand tokens, and a statement over 1.5 million characters cannot be sent at all.
- "Checked by Lean's kernel" is true of one of the two proofs. `bv_decide` trusts compiled code.

## Decisions
- A query closed with the model, so criterion 7 asks for an ADR proposal and its ticket: ADR 0051
  (proposed) and P1-039. The evidence does not ask for the build, so ADR 0051's decision 7 and
  P1-039's first criterion are a gate: nothing is built until a proof closes a query no configured
  solver decides, measured after P1-038 and P1-033.
- No ticket for the tactics' idea in Z3: the scaffold alone closed nothing.
- No README scoreboard number moves: nothing under `src/` changed.
