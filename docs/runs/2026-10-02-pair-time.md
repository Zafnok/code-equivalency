# P2-076 pair time: gitextensions-8522 (2026-10-02)

Question: where does a pair's time go when it is not the solver using its budget, and does removing
that time cost a verdict?

**Answer: two stages that are not solver queries cost the square of a pair's size: `Inline`'s
substitution and the disposal of the query's Z3 context. Made linear, they take 1,520 s and about
770 s of the `verify` phase down to 31 s and 40 s, the phase from 7,016 s to 4,880 s, and the run
from 2h43m to 2h05m.** What is left is the solver spending its own budget (97.5% of `verify`), which
is P2-050's and P2-083's question. No query ran past its timeout, so the interrupt this ticket adds
at four times `timeoutMs` never fired.

**Results: 13,734 of 13,742 are the same as the baseline's, and so are 13,735 of the two runs at the
base commit with each other.** Every result that differs sits on a query the resource limit ends in
some runs and not in others, which P2-082 traced to term ids. Rerun ten times with each build, those
pairs are decided at about the same rate before and after, except one that the base commit decides in
20% of runs and this branch in 8.5%. That pair is the one Deviation in the ticket.

## Setup
- Pair: human, gitextensions/gitextensions PR #8522, legacy 3f4ed21998af, modern 5190ba5c1a5f.
- Builds: the base commit `d135993`; the same with this ticket's stage log added and nothing else
  changed ("measurement"); and this branch at `7cbe2d7` ("after").
- Runs, each `equiv compare` at the default config (`resourceLimit` 5,000,000, `timeoutMs` 60,000) with
  `--verbosity debug`: base and measurement side by side, then after and after-again side by side.
- Machine: one Windows box, 24 logical cores, 63 GB, shared throughout with two to four corpus runs of
  other sessions.
- Repeats: a throwaway harness (not committed) that loads both solutions through the production
  frontend once and calls `Z3Backend.Verify` on chosen pairs, as `compare` does, built against each
  commit.
- The 30 pairs over 30 s in P2-046's run (`docs/runs/2026-09-30-full-gitextensions-8522`) were taken
  from that run's progress log. 14 of them are congruent on today's `main` and never reach the solver,
  `FormRemotes::InitializeComponent()` (126.5 minutes then) among them.

## Where the time goes (criterion 1)

Seconds by stage, summed over every pair of the phase. `other` is a pair's time that no stage line
covers: before the fix the context's disposal, which had no stage line yet, and bookkeeping.

| phase, run | pairs' time | solver queries | inline | dispose | other | everything else |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| verify, measurement | 7,016 | 4,679 | 1,520 | (in other) | 774 | 43 |
| verify, after | 4,880 | 4,760 | 31 | 40 | 16 | 33 |
| verify, after again | 4,995 | 4,869 | 31 | 46 | 17 | 33 |
| contracts, measurement | 2,616 | 2,448 | 103 | (in other) | 58 | 7 |
| contracts, after | 2,468 | 2,424 | 4 | 10 | 26 | 4 |

- Of the 3,519 queries of the measurement run, 1,969 answered sat (longest 13.5 s), 1,080 unsat
  (longest 13.4 s) and 470 unknown (longest 60.1 s). No query answered sat or unsat after more than
  `timeoutMs`, and none ran past it: the 470 unknowns are the resource limit or the 60 s backstop. The
  after runs agree (longest sat 14.1 s, longest unsat 22.4 s, longest unknown 60.1 s).
- The stage that cost the square of a pair's size was `Inline`: it handed Z3 every definition so far
  for every definition it substituted. The other was disposal: `Inline` left a .NET object holding
  each inlined definition until the finalizer ran, after the context was disposed, and Z3 frees the
  terms a disposed context still holds one layer of nesting per pass over all of them. On a
  straight-line pair of 4,000 calls rung 1 took 96 s, 10.6 s of it in `Inline` and 85 s in disposal;
  it now takes 0.9 s.
- Encoding (`unroll` and `encode` together, 19 s over the whole `verify` phase), model decoding (16 s)
  and the steps after the ladder are not where the time goes. Failure refinement is mostly its own
  solver queries.
